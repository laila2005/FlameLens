| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Hook: fire has no "up" in space (reuse the video opener) |
| 0:20 | Pick "Lunar rover, 10.2 psi, 34% O₂". Ranked cards appear with score bars |
| 0:50 | Open the Evidence case: "3 of 5 conditions proven, nothing matches the whole design". Click a run ID to show its source |
