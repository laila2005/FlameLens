## Who comes onsite

- A team of 6 may have **only 5 members onsite**. The sixth joins virtually, on Discord. Decide who before Thursday (open decision, owner: the team).
- Onsite: bring national ID, laptop, charger. Wi-Fi is free but shared and may be congested, so **bring a backup internet connection**.
- You may leave overnight and return by 8:00 AM Saturday, but tell the registration desk and check in again. Rest rooms exist, sleeping facilities do not, so bring a sleeping bag.
- No companions. No laptops are provided.
- Virtual team members: stay on Discord, follow the announcements channel, and attend mentorship sessions online. The camera need not be on, and Discord need not be open all the time, but follow updates.

## Mentorship

Mentors guide the approach and give feedback. They **cannot write code** or finish the solution. Requests go through Discord. Tracks include AI and machine learning, data science and analytics, web development, software engineering, UI/UX, storytelling, video editing, art and visualization. Overnight support may be limited.

## Judging criteria (NASA, same worldwide)

| Criterion | What judges look for | How FlameLens answers it |
|---|---|---|
| Impact | The problem, who benefits, the difference it makes | Engineers get a defensible answer in a minute instead of days of reading, and learn what to test next |
| Creativity | Original approach or meaningful improvement | Evidence case, next experiment, strength meter, instead of search and chat |
| Validity | Sound science, reliable data, feasible, supports claims | Every claim cites a real run, verifier, confidence labels, "no evidence" when true |
| Relevance | Clear response to the chosen challenge | Built on the PSI data the challenge provides |
| Presentation | Clear explanation of problem, solution, evidence, value | 30-second video or 7 slides, kept simple enough for non-specialists |

Judges are experts from different fields, not necessarily in combustion. Keep the demo clear for non-specialists.

## Submission requirements checklist (official)

- [ ] **Project demo:** 7 slides or a 30-second video, on a publicly accessible platform
- [ ] **Project links:** final project link, GitHub repository, other relevant code links
- [ ] **Project summary:** problem, solution, impact, how it meets the selected challenge
- [ ] **Project resources:** datasets, sources, references, tools used
- [ ] No under-18 images, names or voices in the demo
- [ ] AI use disclosed
- [ ] Submitted by the team leader to the local form, then the same files to the official NASA Space Apps site

## Awards

10 Global Nominees from Cairo, plus Sponsor Awards. Local winner and People's Choice shortlist are announced at the closing ceremony, and shortlisted teams present there. Virtual teams are invited to the ceremony (optional). The Global part is essential: submitting only locally means no global judging.
