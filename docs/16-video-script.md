## Tips for sounding human

- Read it 2 or 3 times out loud before you record, until you know it almost by heart. Then look up from the paper and *tell* it.
- Stress a few words: "couldn't **forget**", "**no one** thought was dangerous", "lesson **alive**", "every result **cited**", "a **family** waiting".
- Don't act sad. Speak simply and honestly; that is what moves people.
- Each take should be about 29 seconds. If it runs long, say "in a routine test" instead of "in a test no one thought was dangerous".
