The narration in one block, for reading practice:

> Hi, I'm Laila, Team AstroSpark. We chose Flame in Freefall because of Apollo 1.
> Pure oxygen. One spark. The hatch wouldn't open. Three astronauts never came home.
> We built FlameLens so their lesson is never lost.
> Enter your cabin's air. FlameLens checks eighteen NASA fire experiments: what's proven, what's missing, the closest real test.
> For every engineer building on the Moon or Mars, and every family waiting on Earth: every crew deserves to come home.

## How to read it

- **The story (0:05–0:11):** slow and quiet. Leave a real pause after each sentence. This is the moment the judges should feel.
- **The message (0:11–0:14):** steady and sure. This is the turn from grief to purpose.
- **How it works (0:14–0:22):** a little faster and brighter. It's the part about the solution.
- **The closing (0:22–0:30):** warm and slow, slowest on "every crew deserves to come home". Then stop.
- Each full take should be 28–29 seconds. If it's longer, cut "Team" in the first line, then "the closest real test".

## What Laila records and sends

1. **The voice:** 3 full takes, with 1 second of silence before each. Quiet room, no fan or AC running, phone about 20 cm from your mouth.
2. **Optional, for 0:00–0:02:** a landscape clip of you saying the first line on camera, at eye level, with a plain background.
3. Send the original phone files, not WhatsApp copies, or put them in `media/raw-voice/`. Claude then edits everything else.

## Why it is written this way

- **The story is told in the spoken words, not only on screen:** oxygen, a spark, a trapped crew, three lives.
- **The message links the story to the product.** Apollo 1 is why NASA tests how materials burn in oxygen-rich air. FlameLens makes that knowledge easy to find, so "their lesson is never lost".
- **The impact is human, not technical.** It speaks to the engineers who design the cabins and the families who wait on Earth, and it ends on the crew coming home, which answers "never came home".
- **No safety promise.** FlameLens shows the evidence and the gaps; the script never says "FlameLens makes it safe".
